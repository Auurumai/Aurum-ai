# AURUM AI

## Institutional Gold Intelligence Platform

AURUM AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help traders analyze Gold (XAUUSD) using advanced technical analysis, computer vision, and explainable artificial intelligence.

## Vision

To build one of the world's most advanced AI platforms for Gold market analysis.

## Features

- Chart Image Analysis
- Candlestick Recognition
- Gann Square of 9
- Gann Square 144
- Smart Money Concepts
- ICT Concepts
- Market Structure
- Elliott Wave
- Wyckoff
- Fibonacci
- AI Consensus Engine
- Risk Management
- Explainable AI
